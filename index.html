What is TeacherBuddy?

A short setup, then it runs quietly in the background of your school day.

TeacherBuddy listens through the school day, then turns it into a short, honest reflection — achievements, anecdotes, and the moments that pulled you off track — so you can see your own day clearly and talk it through, one week at a time.

Everyone's audio is handled with care: it's processed only to build these summaries, and student identities never leave this device.

Version history

This build: v2.3 — finalized · Sep 22, 2026. Voice enrollment and check-in voice notes use your real microphone. Diarization itself (telling speakers apart) is still simulated — that needs a backend speech model this static prototype doesn't have.
v2.3Sep 22, 2026

Added a live microphone status indicator and an "Enable microphone" button on the Diarization page, so you can trigger the browser's permission prompt on demand and see plainly whether the mic is allowed, blocked, or not yet requested. Error messages now explain that changed browser settings often need a page reload, and that a mic block inside an embedded chat preview usually means opening this page in its own full browser tab. Also updated "A rough sense of your week" to hour-range dropdowns (0, 1–2, 3–5, 6–8, 9+ hrs) with a guiding time-management question per category, replacing Low/Medium/High.

v2.2Sep 22, 2026

Redesigned in navy + Helvetica for a sleeker, simpler look. Restructured setup into four steps — About, Your Details, Onboarding, Diarization — followed by a daily Live the Day / Reflection tab pattern running Monday through Friday, each ending with a Submit action, plus a separate Weekly Reflection for the weekend. Live the Day now opens with a big Start/Stop Recording button and a live timer, with on-screen guidance: start recording when the day begins, stop before heading home, and spend at least 5 minutes in Reflection afterward.

v2.1Sep 17, 2026

Added an editable teacher profile (name, subject/grade, school).

v2.0Sep 17, 2026

Finalized build: real microphone recording via getUserMedia/MediaRecorder for the voice-enrollment samples and check-in voice notes, with playback and a clear on-screen message if the mic is denied or blocked.

Earlier structural history (six-tab prototype → linear wizard) matches the mockup's v1.0–v1.3 — see that build's version history.
